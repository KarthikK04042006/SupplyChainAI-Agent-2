Cover per cell is the SKU’s average daily demand over the last 90 days, split evenly across its stocking locations. Below lead time plus the review period counts as at risk; above the overstock threshold counts as overstocked. So a cell reads short when it holds less than an equal share of the network’s stock, which assumes the locations serve comparable demand — demand is not recorded per warehouse. This is an allocation estimate computed on this page, not a prediction from the forecasting service, and it can disagree with the alert list.
